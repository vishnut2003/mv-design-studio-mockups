Architect and Auditor

One principal, two roles: architect and auditor.

Most projects have an architect on one side and, if you are lucky, an independent auditor on the other. At MV Design Studio the principal holds both roles, so design intent, cost and compliance are checked by the one person who knows the project best.

Cost protection

BOQs, rates and bills checked against drawings before you pay.

Quality on site

Stage-wise site audits of structure, services and finishes.

Compliance

Bye-laws, approvals and documentation handled and verified.

One accountable person

No gap between the design team and the people who check the work.
