Architect and Auditor

For the reasons why the architect needs to be the auditor.

On most projects the designer leaves after the drawings are issued and the contractor measures and certifies the work himself. Our principal remains on as auditor. Before sending a payment, the quantity, workmanship and approvals are compared to the drawings by the person who prepared the drawings.

Cost under control

Rates, quantities and running bills checked prior to payments.

Workmanship checked

Inspection of structure, services and finishes in stages.

Approvals in order

Track and verify the compliance of byebaws, conditions of sanction and completion papers.

One point of answer

The person who drew it will be held responsible for how it has been constructed.
