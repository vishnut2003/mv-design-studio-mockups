Our Process

From first call
to handover.

Seven steps, two approval gates and audit checkpoints through construction. This page answers the questions clients ask before they call.

Step by Step

How a project runs

The same path for every project, whether we are designing the building, the interior, or auditing someone else's work. Durations are placeholders and depend on the project and the authority.

Approval authority sign-off required   Audit checkpoint principal inspects and reports
01

First call & brief

A call within 24 hours, then a site visit. We listen to what you want to build, look at the plot or space, and agree a scope and fee.

You receive · Written brief · fee proposal
02

Site visit & feasibility

We study the plot, the bye-laws and the budget to confirm what can be built and how much it should cost.

You receive · Feasibility note · FAR and setback study
03

Concept design

Layouts, massing and the look of the building or interior, refined with you over two or three rounds.

You receive · Plans · 3D views · material direction
04

Design development & approvals Approval

Drawings are detailed and the sanction set is submitted. We follow the approval through until the project is cleared to build.

You receive · Sanction drawings · approval
05

Working drawings & tendering

Structural, services and detail drawings, specifications and a BOQ that contractors can price. We help you compare bids.

You receive · Working drawing set · BOQ · contractor recommendation
06

Execution with audit checkpoints Audit checkpoint

The principal inspects the site at each stage, checks bills against the BOQ and issues a written report after every visit.

You receive · Stage reports · verified bills · snag lists
07

Handover Audit checkpoint

Completion documents, a final audit report and a walk-through. We stay on call for support after you move in.

You receive · Completion certificate support · final audit report

Before You Call

Questions clients ask first

Answers are placeholder copy for layout review and will be confirmed with MV Design Studio.

Ask Us Anything
How much does it cost to get started?
The first call and site visit are free. Fees are quoted after the brief, as a percentage of project cost or a lump sum per stage, and agreed before design begins. (placeholder answer)
Do I need a contractor before I come to you?
No. We design first, prepare a BOQ and then help you tender to contractors, or recommend ones we have worked with. (placeholder answer)
How long before I can start building?
Design takes eight to twelve weeks; approvals depend on the authority. We prepare a complete sanction submission to avoid avoidable rounds. (placeholder answer)
What if I already have drawings?
We review them as a project audit, flag issues and either take the project forward or support your existing team. (placeholder answer)
How involved do I need to be?
You approve each stage before the next begins. Between approvals, the principal runs the project and reports to you after every site visit. (placeholder answer)
