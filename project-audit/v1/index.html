What It Is

Project audit by a practising architect.

If someone else is designing or building your project, we check it for you. We review the drawings and specifications, inspect the site at agreed stages, verify quantities and bills, and confirm the work complies with the sanctioned plan and the bye-laws. Every visit ends in a written report.

  • Design and drawing review before construction
  • Stage-wise site and quality inspections
  • Bill and quantity checks against BOQ
  • Compliance checks against sanctioned plans and bye-laws
  • Written report and action list after every visit

Who It Is For

Owners building through a contractor

You have a design and a contractor and want someone on your side checking the work and the bills.

A flat, villa or plot you are about to take over, inspected for quality, completeness and compliance.

Developers & institutions

Third-party audit of drawings, BOQs and site execution for group housing, schools and institutional blocks.

Ask Us Anything
When should an audit start?
Ideally before construction, so the drawings and BOQ are reviewed first. An audit can also start mid-way through a project or before possession. (placeholder answer)
Will you work with my existing architect or contractor?
Yes. The audit is independent of both. We share findings with you first and then with your team in a way that keeps the project moving. (placeholder answer)
How often do you visit the site?
At agreed construction stages, typically every two to four weeks, or more often for critical stages such as slab casting and waterproofing. (placeholder answer)
What does the report contain?
Observations with photographs, deviations from drawings or bye-laws, cost or quantity discrepancies, and a clear action list with responsibilities. (placeholder answer)
