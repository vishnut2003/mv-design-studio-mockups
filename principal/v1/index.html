Architect and Auditor

How the dual role protects you

On most projects the architect draws, the contractor builds and nobody independently checks the two against each other. Here the person who drew it is also the person who checks it.

On cost

Quantities and bills are verified against the drawings before payment. Variations are priced and approved in writing.

On quality

Stage-wise site inspections of structure, services and finishes, with photographs and a written report after every visit.

On compliance

Bye-laws, sanction conditions and completion requirements verified at each stage, so there are no surprises at occupancy.
