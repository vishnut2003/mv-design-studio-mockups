What It Is

Interior design, concept to completion.

We plan every room around light, movement and material, then detail lighting, joinery, finishes and furniture in drawings a contractor can price and build from. The principal checks the work on site at every stage.

  • Space planning and furniture layouts
  • Material, finish and colour palettes
  • Lighting design and electrical layouts
  • Joinery, wardrobe and kitchen drawings
  • Site supervision, snagging and styling

Who It Is For

Is this the right service for you?

New homes

A new apartment, villa or farmhouse shell that needs to be planned, detailed and finished as one coherent interior.

Workplaces

Offices, clinics and showrooms where layout, lighting and acoustics change how a team works.

Renovations

An existing home or space that needs re-planning and a complete refresh without starting from scratch.

How a full project runs
01

Brief & measure

We measure the space and understand how you live or work in it.

  • Measured drawings
  • Lifestyle or workflow brief
  • Budget range and fee proposal
02

Concept

Layouts, mood and material direction for every room.

  • Furniture layout options
  • Mood boards and 3D views
  • Material and colour palette
03

Design development

Each room detailed down to lighting and joinery.

  • Lighting and electrical layouts
  • Ceiling, flooring and wall finishes
  • Joinery and kitchen drawings
04

Documentation

Drawings and schedules contractors can price and build from.

  • Working drawing set
  • Specifications and BOQ
  • Vendor shortlist
05

Execution & styling

Stage inspections, snagging and the final dressing.

  • Stage-wise site audits
  • Snag list and sign-off
  • Styling and handover

Ask Us Anything
Do you supply furniture and materials?
We design and specify everything and shortlist vendors. Purchases are made in your name at vendor rates, with our team checking quality on delivery. (placeholder answer)
How long does an interior project take?
Design takes four to eight weeks depending on size. Execution usually runs three to six months, with inspections at each stage. (placeholder answer)
Can you design only a few rooms?
Yes. Many clients start with the living areas or a kitchen and extend the scope later. (placeholder answer)
Do you work with my contractor?
Yes. We supervise and audit the contractor you choose, and check bills against the drawings before you pay. (placeholder answer)
