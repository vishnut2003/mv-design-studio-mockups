The Brief

A compact plot, built upwards.

  • Built-up area 5,200 sq ft · placeholder
  • Programme 4 bedrooms, family lounge, roof terrace, basement
  • Structure RCC frame, brick infill, stone and timber cladding
  • Approvals Sanctioned 2022 · completion 2024
  • Audit 14 stage inspections · 0 open snags

The clients wanted a family home for three generations on a 200 square yard plot, with the privacy of a house and the outlook of an apartment. The plan stacks a floor per family, with a shared entrance lobby, a basement for parking and services, and a planted terrace under a cantilevered roof canopy. (placeholder brief)

The street elevation is a deep grey marble frame holding timber-clad volumes, perforated metal screens and glass. The screens and planters cut west sun on the bedrooms, while the double-height entrance and the corner glazing bring light into the stair.

The principal prepared the sanction and working drawings, then inspected the site at fourteen stages from foundation to finishes. Contractor bills were verified against the BOQ before each payment, and the house was handed over with no open snags.
