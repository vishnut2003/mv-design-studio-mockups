Ask Us Anything
How long does a typical project take?
A full home interior usually takes four to six months from first consultation to handover, depending on size and scope. Architecture projects run longer because of approvals and construction. We agree a detailed schedule before any work begins. (placeholder answer)
How do you handle budgets?
Every project starts with an itemised estimate broken down by room and by trade. Costs are agreed before execution, and any change is priced and approved by you in writing before it goes ahead. (placeholder answer)
Which cities do you work in?
We are based in the Delhi NCR region and regularly deliver projects across North India. For projects elsewhere, we assess site logistics during the first consultation. (placeholder answer)
Do you take care of municipal approvals?
Yes. For architecture projects we prepare and submit the drawings and documentation required for sanction, and coordinate with the authorities until approval is received. (placeholder answer)
Can I hire you for design only, without execution?
Absolutely. Architecture, interior design and furniture design are each available as standalone services. Many clients begin with design and add turnkey execution once the drawings are ready. (placeholder answer)
What happens after handover?
We walk through the finished space with you, close any snags and hand over care instructions and warranties. Our team stays on call for support long after you move in. (placeholder answer)
