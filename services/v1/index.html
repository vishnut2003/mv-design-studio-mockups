Why It Matters

Every service comes with an auditor's eye

Because the principal also works as an independent project auditor, every MV Design Studio service is delivered with the checks an auditor would run: cost, quality and compliance.

Cost

Quantities, rates and contractor bills checked against the drawings before you pay.

Quality

Stage-wise inspections of structure, services and finishes, recorded in written reports.

Compliance

Bye-laws, sanction conditions and documentation verified at each approval stage.

Ask Us Anything
Can I hire you for design only, without site supervision?
Yes. Architecture and interior design are available as design-only engagements, and you can add site supervision or a stage-wise audit at any point. (placeholder answer)
What does a project audit actually cover?
A design review of drawings and specifications, site inspections at agreed stages, checks of quantities and bills against the drawings, and a written report after every visit. (placeholder answer)
Do you take care of municipal approvals?
Yes. For architecture projects we prepare and submit the drawings and documentation required for sanction, and coordinate with the authorities until approval is received. (placeholder answer)
How are fees structured?
Design services are quoted as a percentage of project cost or as a lump sum per stage. Audit services are quoted per visit or as a monthly retainer, depending on the project. (placeholder answer)
Which cities do you work in?
We are based in Delhi NCR and regularly work across North India. For projects elsewhere, we assess site logistics during the first consultation. (placeholder answer)
