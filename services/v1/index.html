Why It Matters

All services are subject to the same checks.

The principal conducts audits on other projects too. The three checks applied to those audits are utilized for each project that the studio designs: cost, quality and compliance.

Cost

Comparisons of quantities, rates and running bills with the drawings prior to payment.

Quality

Inspection and documentation of structure, services and finishes throughout.

Compliance

As work progresses, confirmed by Bye-laws, Sanction conditions and completion papers.
